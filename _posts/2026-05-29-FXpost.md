I recently received a "Backbone" scholarship from the American Wildfire Experience and Mystery Ranch for the following submission to their biannual essay contest.

I wrote it as a resource for folks who are new to fire and might not know about these kind of jobs. 

One of the cool things I discovered about wildland firefighting is that there is so many different flavors of firefighter beyond your conventional handcrew/hotshot. Whether you want to rappel out of a helicopter (crazy!) or nerd out over science (me), or just want to sit in a truck all day (sorry engine slugs <3), there's a place for you somewhere, you just have to know what to look for!

Big thank you to my friend and mentor Matt Biggs, who first told me about wildland fire modules and fire effects crews back when I was in AmeriCorps. Wanted to pay his kindness forward :).
